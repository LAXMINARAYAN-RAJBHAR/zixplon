About ZIXPLON

The video and social platform at zixplon.in

ZIXPLON is an Indian video-sharing and social platform for creators and viewers. You can watch videos and reels without an account. Sign in with your email or your Google account to upload, comment, follow creators and message other people.

Open ZIXPLON

What you can do on ZIXPLON

  • Upload and stream videos and reels
  • Share posts and photo or video stories that disappear after 24 hours
  • Subscribe to creators, like, comment and share
  • Send direct messages and make voice calls
  • Search by text or voice and get notifications

Signing in with Google

When you choose "Sign in with Google", ZIXPLON receives your Google name, email address and profile picture. We use them only to create your account, sign you in and show your profile. We never access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or calendar. Details are in our Privacy Policy.
