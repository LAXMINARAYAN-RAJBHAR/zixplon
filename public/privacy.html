Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026. Effective immediately.

ZIXPLON ("we", "us") operates the ZIXPLON video streaming and social platform at https://zixplon.in. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use and share it, and your rights.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: username, email address, profile picture and bio when you register or sign in.
  • Google Sign-In data: if you choose "Sign in with Google", we receive your Google account name, email address and profile picture (the openid, email and profile permissions). We do not access your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, calendar or any other Google data.
  • Content you create: videos, posts, stories, comments, messages, thumbnails, titles and descriptions.
  • Usage data: pages visited, videos watched, search queries, likes, subscriptions and visit duration.
  • Device information: browser type, operating system, IP address and approximate location (country).
  • Notification data: if you enable push notifications, your browser's push subscription.
  • Payments: payment features are processed by our payment partner (Razorpay). We do not store your card or UPI details.

2. How we use your information

  • To create your account, sign you in, and show your name and profile picture
  • To provide, personalise and improve the platform and your content recommendations
  • To send notifications about your account and activity
  • To enable messages and voice calls between users
  • To detect and prevent abuse, spam and illegal content
  • To comply with legal obligations under Indian law

3. Google user data

We use your Google name, email address and profile picture only to create your ZIXPLON account, sign you in, and display your profile. We do not sell this data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the service providers listed below that we need to run the platform. ZIXPLON's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We use these service providers to run ZIXPLON, and they may process your data on our behalf:

  • Supabase: database and authentication
  • Vercel: hosting and deployment
  • Cloudflare: DNS, security and media storage and delivery
  • LiveKit: voice calls
  • Razorpay: payments
  • Google: sign-in
  • Giphy: GIFs
  • DiceBear and UI Avatars: default profile pictures
  • ipapi.co and ipwho.is: country detection (no data stored)

We may also disclose data when required by law.

5. Data storage and security

Your data is stored securely with the providers above and may be processed on servers outside India under equivalent data protection standards.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. Stories are removed automatically after 24 hours. You can delete your account, or email us to request deletion of your data, and we will delete it within a reasonable period, normally 30 days.

7. Cookies and local storage

We use browser local storage to keep you signed in and remember your preferences. We may use analytics cookies in future and will disclose them in an updated policy.

8. Your rights

  • Request access to the data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account
  • Lodge a complaint with India's data protection authority

9. Children's privacy

ZIXPLON is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has registered, contact us and we will remove the account.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy and will notify registered users of significant changes. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, email support@zixplon.in.
